Restore your full game

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. A connection is required. The full game is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Worlds and progression

Purchasing unlocks access to Center, Station and endless modes. In-game light and level requirements still apply. Harbor is not part of the current playable campaign.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, app version, level name and what happened. A screenshot can help. Never send card details.

Refunds

Apple handles payment and refund requests through Report a Problem.
