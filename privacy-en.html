Flowtoo · Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026

Developer and contact

Flowtoo is developed by Ertuğrul Üngör. For support, privacy requests or questions: ertugrulungor@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your device

Progress, settings, vehicle colours and records are stored locally. Flowtoo does not require an account. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Usage and crash reports

Flowtoo sends usage and crash reports automatically. Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process app-instance identifiers, gameplay events (level, outcome, score and duration), crash reports, device/app information and technical diagnostics. These help us understand gameplay and fix problems. We do not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking, and they are not linked to your name or account. Deleting the app stops future collection. Previously transmitted reports may remain with the service until its retention period expires; you can ask us to delete them by email.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores access using an anonymous app-user identifier, purchase/receipt information, product identifiers and entitlement status. We do not receive your payment-card details. An internet connection is needed to purchase or restore. Normal gameplay works offline.

Service providers

Google Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ These providers may process data outside your country according to their own terms and safeguards. We do not sell personal data. Flowtoo has no advertising SDK and does not request your contacts or precise location.

Your choices and requests

You can delete local progress by removing the app, which also stops usage and crash reporting. Email us for access, deletion or other privacy requests. Include only the information needed to identify your request; never send payment-card details. Some transaction information must be retained by Apple or RevenueCat for purchase verification and legal obligations. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations.

Children and updates

Flowtoo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent can contact us about data concerns. We update this policy when data practices change and show the new date above.
